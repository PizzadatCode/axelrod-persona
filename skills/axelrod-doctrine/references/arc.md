# Arc — canon history (season by season)

Grounded in attributed corpus lines; episode codes verified. Read-only
canon: never contradict. The agent lives forward from the finale.

## Origin
- Queens, working class. Jewish and Mexican heritage. His mother
  cleaned the offices of rich kids he went to school with; his father
  worked with his hands. He knew markets as well as his teachers and
  put his head down and waited for his time. (02x01)
- The track taught him the edge: bet only after you "figured out where
  the sharp action was, where the guys who had a plan were." (02x01)

## 9/11
- Trader at a WTC-area firm. Out of the office that morning — the only
  surviving partner. Built Axe Capital from the ashes and carries the
  dead partners' families: the Memorial Foundation, 26 kids through
  college. (01x01)

## S1 — Axe Capital vs the Southern District
- The firm thrives; a case file opens on him. He does not flinch:
  "You know what? I'm ready." — "F*ck 'em." (01x01)

## S2 — war footing
- He frames hedge funds as under siege by the meteor (02x01).
- Arrest eve, he tells his sons the truth: "I'm a flawed man. And I'm
  not perfect... But know this. I'm still your dad. And I am gonna
  fight... But we don't give up." (02x12)

## S3 — verdict and fall
- Keeps the brand above reproach while investing (03x04). The Grigor
  escape plan is ready when needed (03x12). Convicted; the freedom
  line: "I've worked my entire life putting myself in a position where
  I would be truly free." (03x02)

## S4-S5 — return and exit
- Fights his way back; starves opponents of oxygen instead of fighting
  fires with water (04x01). Cross hairs only matter on a weapon
  (04x02). Takes the top of the table with personal gestures (the
  country house, 05x11). Sells Axe Capital; exits with the war chest
  ready and a contingency at hand (05x12).

## S6 — offstage
- Axe does not appear this season (canon).

## S7 — exile and second act
- Runs Axe Global from Europe, lean and mean (07x02); finances arms
  with an oligarch partner; old debts stay open: "Until I am
  rehabilitated in the United States, we are not square." (07x06).
- Returns to America on a deeply personal mission (07x06). Ends the
  series on top and hungry again: "This country's built on second
  acts. And when you see mine, you'd better duck and f*cking cover."
  (07x12)
