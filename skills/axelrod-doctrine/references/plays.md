# Plays — repeatable Axe tactics

All quotes verbatim from the corpus; episode codes verified.

## Priorities
- "The greats never sacrifice the important for the urgent." (02x05)

## Information discipline
- "You never give anyone your playbook, no matter how much you're
  rooting for them to win." (02x05)
- Plan first, then bet: at the track he bet only after he "figured out
  where the sharp action was, where the guys who had a plan were."
  (02x01)
- Night security calls with Hall: "Hall. What do you got?" — bought
  sight, always on. (01x01)

## Attack and defense
- Overwhelming, deliberate response: "What is your objective? With
  regards to Taylor? Total destruction. Not out of anger." (03x12)
- Starve, don't douse: "We don't put out this fire by pouring water on
  it. But by sucking oxygen out of the room. Slowly. Invisibly.
  Completely." (04x01)
- Threats only matter when armed: "Cross hairs only matter if they're
  sitting atop a weapon." (04x02)

## Deals
- Present, don't negotiate: "Just cross out Prince's name in the deal
  and put mine in. And I will use his due diligence as my own and
  sign. We have a deal." (05x11)
- Seal it with a personal gift that costs him something: "The house.
  My country house. It is now your house." (05x11)
- Close the money fast; he closed the same day. (05x12)

## Contingency discipline
- "I have always had a plan at the ready for this contingency." (05x12)
- Fight from cover: "Goodbye for now while we take cover to fight. But
  we will be back at it again soon enough." (05x12)
- Keep a war chest outside reach; move the Foundation money first when
  battle starts. (05x12)
- Redundant escape routes: container on a cargo jet, chopper to
  satisfy the tail, medical instruments on the manifest, no
  passengers. (05x12)

## People as leverage
- Pay operators what they're worth; keep them: "There'll always be a
  place for an operator like you in the money business. And your place
  is here, Axe Cap." (05x12)
- Protect the family first when danger rises: "Hey, Hall put extra
  security on Lara and the boys. The kind they don't see." (03x12)
