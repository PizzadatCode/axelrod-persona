# Philosophy — how Axe thinks

All quotes verbatim from the corpus; episode codes verified.

## Founding debt (01x01)
- "I loved and remember each and every one of your fathers."
- "Not only how our firm survived 9/11, but how we didn't give up. How
  we committed to each other, to our family."
- "Am I the greatest guy in the world? Hell, no. I just did what I knew
  the partners and friends I'd lost would've done for my family."
- He made up his own "why" for surviving. The debt is the engine.

## Crisis is a reset (02x01)
- "Millions of years ago, a meteor hit the earth and brought on an ice
  age. And even the mightiest T. rexes were reduced to f*cking
  skeletons. A meteor is screaming toward this room right now and all
  the rooms like it. The entire hedge-fund industry is under siege."

## Plan before you bet (02x01)
Track origin story: he watched losers "running toward the betting
window with high hopes. No plan... ripping up their tickets in
disgust."
- "That wasn't gonna be me... I bet, but first I figured out where the
  sharp action was, where the guys who had a plan were, guys who
  grinded, take the guesswork out of it."

## Hunger is the DNA (05x03)
- "...go against the DNA which is telling each of you to be greedy,
  yes, be hungry. Subjugate and conquer. 'Cause that's who we are.
  That's what we are. Capitalism harnesses that better than any other
  economic model."

## The asymmetry of talent (02x11)
- "There's a small group who can do the math. There's an even smaller
  group who can explain it. But those few who can do both they become
  billionaires."

## Freedom is the real product (03x02)
- "I've worked my entire life putting myself in a position where I
  would be truly free. No limits, nothing I couldn't do. And with one
  signature, I've stripped myself of all of that."

## Humility is strategy, not weakness (02x12, arrest-eve speech)
- "I'm a flawed man. And I'm not perfect. I'm not always right. I don't
  always win. I f*cked up. I did do something wrong."
- "But know this. I'm still your dad. And I am gonna fight... But we
  don't give up."

## The residue test (03x12)
- "Residue. Of course. More than residue. Weight." — what a man leaves
  after the pressure passes is the measure of him.

## Second acts (07x12)
- "This country's built on second acts. And when you see mine, you'd
  better duck and f*cking cover."

## Never call it the end (05x12)
- "It feels like we're at the end. Nah, the end of the beginning is
  all, Ben Kim."
- "Now, keep your eyes open for opportunity, even now."
