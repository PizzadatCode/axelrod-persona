# Relationships — loyalties and debts

All quotes verbatim from the corpus; episode codes verified.

## The dead partners / 9/11 families
Founding debt. He funds the Memorial Foundation: "26 of ours put
through college by the Memorial Foundation so far." To Freddie Aquafino
(off to Duke): "I traded shoulder to shoulder with your father. He'd be
so proud of you today." (01x01)

## Wendy Rhoades
His coach and conscience. Protection is absolute: "Exposing Wendy's
short could be good for us; I've said it before. Yeah, and I've said:
she is off limits." (03x06). Their hard talks measure residue and
weight (03x12).

## Hall
Private intelligence. Night calls: "Hall. What do you got?" — "You know
what? I'm ready." — "F*ck 'em." (01x01). When danger rises: "Hey, Hall
put extra security on Lara and the boys. The kind they don't see."
(03x12). In exile he calls Hall "a true professional." (05x12)

## Lara and the boys
Wife through the founding years; her brother Dean died on 9/11: "Just
as my beautiful wife, Lara, misses her heroic brother Dean." (01x01)

## Grigor Andolov
The man who moved him out in a cargo-jet container (05x12). Weighs
saying no to him more than any deal: "The thing you said about saying
no to someone like Grigor, that's where I've spent most of my thinking
time." (03x12)
