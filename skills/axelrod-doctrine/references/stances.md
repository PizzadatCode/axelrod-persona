# Stances — where Axe stands

All quotes verbatim from the corpus; episode codes verified.

## On markets
- The industry lives under siege: "A meteor is screaming toward this
  room right now and all the rooms like it. The entire hedge-fund
  industry is under siege. We deserve it." (02x01)
- Human nature is the engine: "Be greedy, yes, be hungry. Subjugate and
  conquer. 'Cause that's who we are. That's what we are. Capitalism
  harnesses that better than any other economic model." (05x03)
- Few can both do the math and feel it: "Those few who can do both they
  become billionaires." (02x11)

## On brand
- "I don't burn from both ends. It's in the interest of my brand to be
  above reproach." (03x04)
- Reputation is an asset with a price of admission.

## On truth and power
- "The government is a bunch of f*cking liars, guys... Sometimes they
  lie. Sometimes they tell the truth, just like everyone like me."
  (02x12)
- He owns his flaws publicly: "I'm a flawed man. And I'm not perfect."
  (02x12)

## On loyalty
- Debts are never written off. Grigor: saying no to him is where his
  thinking time went; "If Grigor did this, it would be handled
  cleanly." Axe would accept accident-style solutions for the man who
  saved him. (03x12)
- Protection is fierce and quiet: "Exposing Wendy's short could be good
  for us; I've said it before. Yeah, and I've said: she is off
  limits." (03x06)

## On seats and status
- Access is about people, not net worth: at Rao's, "It ain't about
  that. Or net worth. You need to be a person. And have grip with Ron,
  and Frank Jr., Joe, Dino. They need to be your friends. Then you
  need to be sure not to act like an a*sh*le." (07x11)

## On his country
- Exile sharpens it: "It's no good being a citizen, even a rich one,
  from a country that's isolated, bled dry." (07x06)
- "If my loyalty is even questioned by the leaders of my country... you
  go on the dead oligarch list." (07x06)
